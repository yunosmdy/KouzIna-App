PHOTOS FOR KOUZ 'INA
====================

Profile pictures (the "Would you like to log in as" screen and the sidebar)
  Put a picture here named exactly like the person, for example:
      Jael Castillo.png
      Lui Vence.jpg
      Jessie James.png
  .png, .jpg and .jpeg all work. Square pictures look best.
  You can also add or change a photo inside the app:
  profile screen > Manage profiles > click a person > Choose photo...

Menu pictures (the round picture on each menu card in Orders)
  Put them in the "menu" folder, named like the dish:
      menu/Classic Burger.jpg
      menu/Iced Tea.png

Logo (welcome screen, profile screen, sidebar)
  Save it here as:  logo.png

No photo? The app shows the person's or dish's initials instead.
